For results available after 31 July, please apply for admission in the following academic year.&nbsp; General Requirements There is only one intake per academic year here, typically term commences in late July.&nbsp;You must have completed or will be taking&nbsp;High School&nbsp;national examinations in the year of application in order to be considered for admission. All Singapore Citizen and Permanent Resident applicants must be at least 18 years of age as of 1st January of the year of matriculation or have completed a minimum of 12 years of formal education beginning with Primary 1 or equivalent. If you do not meet the requirements, you will need to provide your schooling history from Primary 1 in the form enclosed and upload it to your application for assessment of your eligibility for admission after you have applied for admission.&nbsp; Submission of IELTS (a minimum Overall 6, writing 6, speaking 6) or TOEFL (a minimum of 90, verbal 25)&nbsp;[taken before 2026];-a minimum Overall score of 4.5, speaking 4.5 [TOEFL iBT taken from January 2026 onwards] or SAT (a minimum of 1250 for SAT taken for March 2016 onwards) or Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic) (a minimum of overall 55,speaking 55) or ACT with Writing (a minimum of composite score of 30 in ACT with Writing in one sitting) or AST&nbsp;English score&nbsp;or C1 Advanced [formerly known as Cambridge English Advanced (CAE)- a minimum of Overall 170, writing 170, speaking 170) ] are&nbsp;only compulsory&nbsp;for applicants with PRC Gao Kao qualification (i.e. applicants who will be taking Gao Kao in June 2026 and those who have already taken Gao Kao)&nbsp;OR with Taiwan General Scholastic Ability Test (GSAT) qualification&nbsp;OR with South Korean College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT)/High School Diploma (Ilbankye Kodung Hakkyo) qualification OR with Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education Examination (HKDSE) OR with Vietnam High School Graduation Certificate OR with Thailand Mathayom 6 OR with Indonesian Ijazah SMA OR who do not take up English language in Junior and Senior High School or taken English language only as a 2nd&nbsp;language. For IELTS, TOEFL and PTE Academic, only scores obtained in the&nbsp;2 years leading up to the application deadline will be considered. The validity period for SAT and ACT is 5 years. Submission of any of the English language requirements should follow strictly given deadline per qualification. No late submission is allowed. Minimum Subject Requirements In addition to fulfilling the minimum entry requirements, you must also satisfy the minimum subject requirements for the programmes you apply. Check the&nbsp; minimum subject requirements &nbsp;of the programme you wish to apply. Mother Tongue Language (MTL) Requirement for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents All Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents applying for admission must fulfil any one of the following minimum requirements under MOE&nbsp; mother tongue policy :&nbsp; a grade D7 for Higher MTL at Singapore-Cambridge GCE O-Level; a pass in MTL 'B' or a grade S for H1 MTL or H2 MTL Language and Literature or General Studies in Chinese at Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level, or&nbsp;an MOE-approved MTL-in-lieu*;&nbsp; a pass in MTL A: Literature or MTL A:&nbsp; Language and Literature or Language B MTL at Standard or Higher Level at IB Diploma, or&nbsp;an MOE-approved MTL-in-lieu*; an MOE-approved MTL-exemption*. Students who have not satisfied the requirement above may still apply for admission with no prejudice to their application. Admission will be on a provisional basis and students are required to attain the MTL within the period of their University study. * For students who have been away from Singapore’s school system for some years, the University will submit the application for MTL-in-lieu or MTL-exemption on your behalf if you are offered admission. You will need to provide your academic transcript for the last 2 years of studies prior to university admission for MOE’s assessment. MOE does not accept direct applications. If you have previously been approved a MTL-in-lieu or are exempted from MTL, please upload the MOE approval letter to the ‘Checklist’ after you have submitted your application. Students who have misplaced the letter must notify us at application and request for a replacement from MOE Customer Service at https://www.moe.gov.sg/feedback/ . The letter must be included in the application after receiving from MOE.
