Philosophy, Politics, and Economics (PPE) | School of Social Sciences | NTU Singapore Philosophy, Politics, and Economics (PPE) Programme Philosophy, Politics, and Economics (PPE) is a new integrated Major. Embark on a rigorous education that integrates multiple disciplinary approaches in the study of society, politics, the economy, and ethics in a four-year honours degree program based in the School of Social Sciences (SSS) in partnership with the School of Humanities (SoH). Our programme is designed to cultivate a holistic perspective that fuels innovation and creativity. As a PPE candidate, you shall deep dive into two or three chosen disciplines to acquire substantive knowledge and technical skills, develop professionalism through an internship, and broaden your academic exposure through a one-semester overseas exchange. It is not just an education but a transformative journey where diverse insights converge, sparking creativity and gearing you up for the complexities and challenges of tomorrow. Flexible Specialization Choose a bipartite (two disciplines) or tripartite (three disciplines) structure, culminating in an interdisciplinary final-year project guided by co-supervisors from different departments. Technology-Driven Curriculum Leverage NTU’s strength as a leading technological university with seven integrated themes, including quantitative methods, Singaporean governance, Asian regionalism, and the intersection of science, technology, ethics, and society . Real-World Experience Gain hands-on exposure through compulsory local or overseas internships , plus opportunities for global exchange studies . Comprehensive Learning Complete 134 Academic Units (AUs) , covering Philosophy, Politics, and Economics , along with Interdisciplinary Collaborative Core (ICC) and Broadening & Deepening Electives (BDEs) . Philosophy (PH) The School of Humanities (SoH) offers a Philosophy programme that hones logical reasoning, critical thinking, and analytical skills. This interdisciplinary course connects philosophy with fields like mathematics, physics, economics, and psychology, helping students develop a comprehensive worldview and tackle complex challenges. Public Policy & Global Affairs (PPGA) The PPGA programme at SSS offers interdisciplinary training in politics, international relations, public administration, and policy analysis. Students develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills, preparing them for impactful careers in the public and private sectors across Singapore, Asia, and beyond. Economics (EC) The Economics programme at SSS provides rigorous training in theoretical, empirical, and applied economics. With customizable courses and electives in areas like Internet Economics and Behavioural Economics, students gain strong analytical skills to excel in research, policy, and industry. PPE@SSS: A Pathway to Impactful Careers The PPE degree at NTU equips students with strategic thinking and analytical skills to navigate complex societal and policy challenges. Recognizing that no single discipline can fully capture the intricacies of the social and political world, PPE graduates are trained to tackle pressing issues in governance, economics, and ethics - particularly within the Singaporean and East Asian contexts. As societies progress, policy dilemmas arise - should economic growth take precedence over social equity? These critical questions lie at the heart of philosophy, politics, and economics. PPE graduates harness multidisciplinary insights to make informed decisions that shape the future. With versatile and rigorous training, graduates excel in public service, finance, consulting, research, and education leadership. The programme particularly prepares students for impactful public-service roles, fostering a theoretically informed and civically engaged approach to addressing key challenges in Singapore, Asia, and beyond.
