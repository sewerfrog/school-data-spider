Work-Study Degree Programmes Work-Study Degree Programmes&nbsp;aim to enhance students' employability and readiness for full-time employment by offering multiple on-the-job training experiences throughout their course of study. They do so by providing opportunities for students to alternate between terms of studies and internship work. Students benefit from greater exposure to the professional work environment, allowing them to learn on the job, deepen technical skills, develop soft skills, and cultivate an understanding of real-world practices. Click&nbsp; here &nbsp;for the undergraduate degree programmes that offer the option of a work-study track as part of their curriculum.
