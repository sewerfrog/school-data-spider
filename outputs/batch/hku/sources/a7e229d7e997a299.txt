HONG KONG SUB-DEGREES Admissions to Senior Year Places Admissions to Senior Year Places Every year, we set aside several senior-year places for current students or graduates of a full-time associate degree (AD) or two-year or higher diploma programme (HD) from a community college in Hong Kong. Where entrance requirements are met, final-year students are also eligible to apply provided their AD or HD will be completed by August 2026. We welcome your application through our Direct Admissions Scheme to Senior Year Places. Entrance Requirements Important Dates Fees and Financial Assistance Apply Now Programme Information Programme Information Senior-year applicants may choose up to three programmes. You may also include first-year programmes in your selection. Find out more about our first-year programmes and entrance requirements . Sub-degree students that gain entry to a first-year programme may receive credits towards their graduation based on previous academic results. Entrance Requirements Senior-year applicants will be assessed based on their qualifications and academic achievements irrespective of the community college attended. Please note that meeting the basic university entrance requirements does not guarantee admission, as admission is on a competitive basis. Applicants applying for first-year programmes are expected to meet the relevant entrance requirements. Sports Scholarship Scheme Our Sports Scholarship Scheme offers an alternative path to admission for students with a proven commitment to sporting excellence in addition to their academic pursuits. Faculty of Arts Programme of Study | Programme Requirements . Bachelor of Arts | Sub-degree graduates from all disciplines are eligible. Candidates seeking admission to the major of "Chinese Language and Literature" are required to have achieved a minimum of Level 5 either in Chinese Language or Chinese Literature in the Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) Examination, or an equivalent standard in another public examination. Faculty of Education Programme of Study | Programme Requirements . Bachelor of Science in Information Management | Sub-degree graduates from all disciplines are eligible. Candidates are strongly recommended to take either the IELTS or the TOEFL and good performance in one of the tests would be a great advantage. . Bachelor of Science in Applied Child Development | Sub-degree graduates of a recognised full-time Associate Degree (AD) or Higher Diploma (HD) in Early Childhood Education programme of at least two years in duration from a community college in Hong Kong. . Bachelor of Arts and Sciences in Social Data Science | Sub-degree graduates should be from a relevant discipline, such as Data Science and Analytics, Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, Statistics, Applied Science, Social Sciences. Candidates are required to have achieved an overall band of 7 in the IELTS or equivalent, and a minimum of Level 3 in Mathematics in the HKDSE examination. Faculty of Engineering Programme of Study | Programme Requirements . Bachelor of Engineering | Sub-degree graduates should be from the same or a related discipline.
