Home | Berkeley and University of Hong Kong Dual Degree Program Background image: University of Hong Kong campus building The Dual Degree Program between the University of California, Berkeley and the University of Hong Kong (HKU) offers a unique, international undergraduate experience, comprising of four years spent earning bachelor’s degrees in both Hong Kong and California. Students spend their first two years at HKU, the territory's oldest institute of higher learning and an internationally recognised, research-led comprehensive university. Undergraduate students choose from 10 faculties and 3 schools offering more than 50 programs and over 100 majors to suit their interests. The university's location at the heart of Asia and in one of the world's most vibrant and cosmopolitan cities leads to unique Asian insights into the major challenges faced by the world today. Students complete their last two years at UC Berkeley, the number one public university in the world. Over 23,000 undergraduate students are in the College of Letters and Science, and over 80 degree programs are offered. Berkeley is a global campus and is internationally renowned for excellence and pioneering achievements across all disciplines. Students engage with 170 academic departments and programs, and 80 research units. Berkeley’s professors are highly distinguished researchers and scholars—and leading experts in their fields.
