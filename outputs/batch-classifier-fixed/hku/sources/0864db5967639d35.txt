Talent Search Scheme The Talent Search Scheme is introduced with the aim of recognising the talent of high school students that could not be fully assessed by the national / international examinations alone. It is a “programme-student matching exercise” in essence that provides opportunities for high school students who have demonstrated exceptional talents and interests in specific disciplines / areas to be considered for direct admission to HKU. Nomination Period: 24 September - 1 December 2025 (12:00 noon, Hong Kong Time) Nomination Arrangement Interview and Offer Arrangement Eligibility Eligibility To be eligible for the Talent Search Scheme, students must: a) be a c urrent student pursuing the final year of respective high-school curriculum; and b) apply for admission to HKU through the HKU Application System for Undergraduate Programmes in parallel. Self Nomination Self Nomination > Eligible students with exceptional talents and interests in specific disciplines / areas may self-nominate for consideration under the scheme by ONE of the programmes listed below, which MUST also be their first choice under the HKU Application System for Undergraduate Programmes.
