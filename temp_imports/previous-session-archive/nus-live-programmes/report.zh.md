## 关键官方来源

- [NUS Office of Admissions — Undergraduate Programmes](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/undergraduate-programmes) — Office of Admissions page lists undergraduate programme pathways and says NUS offers 60 majors, 54 second majors, and over 80 minors; A-Z includes Architecture, Business Administration, Computing, Dentistry, Engineering, Humanities & Sciences, Law, Medicine, Music, Nursing, Pharmacy, Pharmaceutical Science, PPE and special programmes.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Programmes index](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/) — The Bulletin programmes index lists NUS faculties/schools/colleges and special, multi-/cross-disciplinary and other academic programmes.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — College of Design and Engineering Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/college-of-design-and-engineering/undergraduate-education/) — CDE Bachelor’s Degree Programmes list includes Architecture, Industrial Design, multiple Engineering majors, Robotics and Machine Intelligence, and Landscape Architecture; degree requirements for cohorts 2025/26 onwards describe a 160-unit curriculum.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — School of Computing Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/school-of-computing/undergraduate-education/) — School of Computing degrees offered include Bachelor of Computing in Computer Science, Information Security, Business Artificial Intelligence Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Bachelor of Science in Business Analytics, and jointly Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering; programmes are four-year undergraduate programmes.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — School of Business Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/school-of-business/undergraduate-education) — NUS Business School offers BBA (Hons), BBA Accountancy (Hons), and BBA Real Estate (Hons); the BBA curriculum lists 9 majors including Accountancy, Applied Business Analytics, Business Economics, Finance, Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Leadership and Human Capital Management, Marketing, Operations and Supply Chain Management, and Real Estate.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Faculty of Science Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/faculty-of-science/undergraduate-education/) — Faculty of Science lists BSc (Hons) majors including Chemistry, Data Science and Analytics, Food Science and Technology, Life Sciences, Mathematics, Pharmaceutical Science, Physics, Quantitative Finance, Statistics, Data Science and Economics, plus Bachelor of Pharmacy / Bachelor of Pharmacy (Honours) and Bachelor of Environmental Studies (Honours).
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Faculty of Arts & Social Sciences Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/faculty-of-arts-and-social-sciences/undergraduate-education/) — FASS undergraduate degrees lead to Bachelor of Arts with Honours or Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours; department degree requirements list 21 subjects including CNM, Economics, English, Geography, Global Studies, History, Philosophy, Political Science, Psychology, Social Work, Sociology, Theatre and Performance Studies and Anthropology.
- [NUS College of Humanities and Sciences — Programmes](https://chs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/) — CHS page lists cross-disciplinary programmes (PPE, Data Science and Economics, Environmental Studies, Geospatial Intelligence) and primary majors; CHS students can read a four-year honours degree leading to BA, BSocSci or BSc.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Faculty of Law Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/faculty-of-law/undergraduate-education/) — The Law Bulletin states that the four-year LLB programme is available full-time and leads to the LLB (Hons) degree; the LLB is a professional degree recognised for admission to practice in Singapore.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/yong-loo-lin-school-of-medicine-including-nursing/undergraduate-education/) — Medicine offers two full-time undergraduate programmes leading to MBBS and BSc Nursing / BSc Nursing (Honours); it also describes BSc Nursing Practice and a career conversion programme for registered nurses.
- [NUS Dentistry — Prospective Students / Undergraduate](https://www.dentistry.nus.edu.sg/ug/prospective-students/) — NUS Dentistry states the four-year full-time Bachelor of Dental Surgery comprises pre-clinical and clinical phases.
- [NUS Dentistry — Education](https://www.dentistry.nus.edu.sg/education/) — NUS Dentistry education page describes a comprehensive four-year Bachelor of Dental Surgery programme recognised by the Singapore Dental Council.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — Yong Siew Toh Conservatory of Music Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/yong-siew-toh-conservatory-of-music/undergraduate-education/) — YST offers the Bachelor of Music (Honours), a four-year full-time undergraduate programme built around one of 20 music majors; students must complete 160 units.
- [NUS Bulletin AY2025/26 — School of Continuing & Lifelong Education Undergraduate Education](https://www.nus.edu.sg/nusbulletin/ay202526/programmes/school-of-continuing-lifelong-education/undergraduate-education/) — SCALE offers BTech Engineering programmes in Chemical, Civil, Electronics, Industrial & Management, Mechanical, and Supply Chain Management, plus Bachelor of Information Technology; BTech is typically completed by working adults on a flexible modular schedule.

## 警告 / 限制

- `crawl_limit_waf`：Simple local HTTP fetches to several NUS hosts returned Incapsula/WAF/noindex challenge pages; source content in this dataset is official but was gathered using browser/search extraction rather than the existing urllib-only MVP crawler.
- `scope_grouped_admission_choices`：NUS OAM admissions choices sometimes group multiple majors under one choice, e.g. Engineering and Humanities & Sciences; this dataset includes both OAM choices and detailed Bulletin/college major records, so some concepts intentionally appear in both grouped and detailed form.
- `no_admission_verdict`：This output is programme information only and does not estimate admission chances or make admissions decisions.
